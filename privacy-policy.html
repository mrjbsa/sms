🎓 Mister JB High School Management System

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Mister JB High School Management System ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when a school's Headmaster, Teachers, or Parents use it. Please read it before connecting your Google account.

1. What this App is

The App is a school management tool that individual schools use to manage students, attendance, marks, fees, homework, and announcements. Each school that registers gets its own independent copy of its data — there is no shared, central database.

2. Where your school's data is stored

All of a school's data (student records, attendance, marks, fee status, homework, announcements, and staff/parent login details) is stored in a single file inside the Headmaster's own Google Drive, created using Google's Drive API. We do not copy this data to any server we operate, and we do not store it anywhere except inside that school's own Google Drive.

The App only ever requests the drive.file Google API scope, which limits it to the one file it itself creates in your Drive — it cannot see, read, or modify any other file already in your Google Drive.

3. What information we (the platform operator) can see

To approve a school's registration, we keep a small separate registration record containing only: the school's name, the Headmaster's Gmail address used to connect, the school's file identifier, and the approval status (pending/approved/denied). We do not have access to student names, marks, attendance, fee amounts, or any other school data — that stays inside the school's own Google Drive, which only the school controls.

4. How Parents and Teachers access data

  • Teachers sign in with their own Google account only if the Headmaster has explicitly granted them edit access to the school's file via Google Drive's own sharing permissions.
  • Parents do not sign in with Google at all. If the Headmaster chooses to enable parent viewing, parents access a read-only, link-based view of their own child's records using a roll number, mobile number, and a password set by the school.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell Google user data, and we do not use it for advertising.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because each school's data lives in that school's own Google Drive, the school's Headmaster is in full control of it. Deleting the data file from Google Drive, or revoking the App's access from your Google Account permissions page, removes the App's access immediately.

7. Children's information

This App is used by schools to manage records of minors (students) on behalf of, and under the direction of, the school and their parents/guardians. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children; all student information is entered by school staff (Headmaster/Teachers) or viewed by parents/guardians. Schools are responsible for obtaining any consents required under local law before entering student information.

8. Security

Access to edit a school's data requires either a Google account explicitly authorised by the Headmaster (via Google Drive's own permission system) or a username/password set by the school for Teacher and Parent logins. We recommend schools only share Parent/Teacher access links and passwords with their own school community.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes are posted means you accept the revised policy.
